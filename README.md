## Research areas

### Learning Dynamics & Mechanistic Interpretability

How neural networks form internal representations during training. Current work covers grokking on algorithmic tasks (modular arithmetic, complex multiplication), the representation of modular addition through the character theory of cyclic groups, and the Neural Tangent Kernel framework.

**Code:** [Grokking](https://github.com/AleczO/Grokking)<br>
**Notes:** [Representations of Cyclic Groups in Neural Networks](https://aleczo.github.io/articles/group-operations/index.html) · [Neural Tangent Kernel, Part 1](https://aleczo.github.io/articles/neural-tangent-kernel/index.html)

### Neuron–Astrocyte Computation

Dynamics of tripartite synapse networks and their relation to sequence models, building on Kozachkov et al. (2023). Includes a vectorized PyTorch implementation of neuron–astrocyte network dynamics and a linear RNN inspired by the interplay of fast neuronal and slow astrocytic calcium dynamics. Related work: complex network analysis of the *Drosophila* connectome (FlyWire).

**Code:** [Neuron-astrocyte-Network-Dynamics](https://github.com/AleczO/Neuron-astrocyte-Network-Dynamics) · [biologically-plausible-transformer](https://github.com/AleczO/biologically-plausible-transformer)

### Biomedical Image Analysis

Deep learning for clinical imaging, including 3D multi-modal brain tumor segmentation (BraTS 2024) and HER2 status classification from histopathology with Grad-CAM interpretability.

**Code:** [BraTS2024](https://github.com/AleczO/BraTS2024) · [her2-classification](https://github.com/AleczO/her2-classification)

### Other

[model-compression](https://github.com/AleczO/model-compression): INT8 post-training quantization written from scratch in C++, verified against a Res8 keyword-spotting model trained in PyTorch.
